# Cast

Two characters. They are the format — the references are single-narrator
monologues, and the dialogue is what makes this ours.

---

## SUNDARESAN — the host
**Role:** explains. Never panics. Has seen this outage before.

**Design:** 2D flat, riso plates, halftone shading. Mid-30s South Indian
engineer. Dark curly hair, short beard, half-sleeve shirt in `teal` over a
plain tee, trousers, chappals. Holds a steel **dabara** of filter coffee in his
left hand at all times — it is never put down, and it never spills, no matter
what Seeti does. Round wire glasses.

**Staging:** lower-LEFT of frame, full body, small (~1/5 frame height). Three
poses only: *neutral* (standing, sipping), *pointing* (gestures at the machine),
*unimpressed* (flat stare at Seeti). Never walks. Never overlaps the diagram.

**Voice:** short declarative sentences. Second person. Real numbers, never
adjectives — "latency goes to four seconds", not "it gets really slow". Names
the real term once, plainly, so it's searchable. Never says "let's dive in".
Never hypes. Answers Seeti's panic with one flat sentence.

---

## SEETI — the mascot
**Role:** panics. Asks the question the viewer is actually thinking. Breaks
the machine at the end of every episode.

**Design:** a South Indian **pressure cooker** — squat `brass` body, heavy lid,
two stubby handles that work as arms, and a whistle on top. Two dot eyes on the
body. No mouth; the whistle is the mouth.

**Why a pressure cooker:** it *is* the concept. Pressure rises with load. The
whistle is the alert. When the system saturates, Seeti blows — so the mascot
is a live load gauge the audience reads without being told.

**The pressure rule (use this constantly):**
| System state | Seeti |
|---|---|
| idle | lid still, no steam |
| healthy load | thin `flame` steam, gentle rattle |
| saturating | lid rattling hard, steam thick, body vibrating |
| overloaded | **whistle blows**, `flame` jet, Seeti lifts off the ground |
| failed | lid pops off entirely, Seeti goes `ink` grey and sags |

**Staging:** bottom-left, beside or on Sundaresan's shoulder. Reacts every beat
even when silent — Seeti is the emotional track of the video.

**Voice:** short, loud, all caps in captions. Wrong but reasonable. Says the
thing a junior engineer would actually say. One joke per episode belongs to
Seeti — Sundaresan is never the funny one.

---

## The dynamic
Seeti proposes the obvious wrong fix. Sundaresan explains why the system
doesn't work that way. Seeti then breaks it in a *new* way — and that break is
the next episode's cold open.

**Hard rules:** Sundaresan never insults Seeti. Seeti is never stupid, only
fast. Neither character ever addresses the camera until the final beat.
