System design, taken apart on a workbench, by an engineer with a filter coffee
and a pressure cooker that will not stop screaming.

## Art direction — "flat cartoon, printed on cheap paper"
The look is **a flat 2D animation cartoon, printed**. Two halves, and both
matter:

**1. The drawing is CARTOON.** Clean uniform-weight black outlines. Flat colour
fills, one solid colour per shape. Simplified chunky graphic forms. Big simple
expressive eyes. Low detail by design, because every frame has to be redrawn —
this is a model sheet for animation, not an illustration.

**2. The print is CHEAP AND WARM.** Cream paper, limited spot inks, light paper
grain, a hair of colour misregistration.

> **Correction, learned the hard way:** calling this a "risograph technical
> manual" produced beautiful *etched engraving* — dense crosshatching, stipple,
> fine line texture. That is the opposite of cartoon. The texture lives in the
> **paper and the ink registration only**, never in the linework. Say "flat
> cartoon" first and "printed" second, never the reverse.

**Banned — rendering:** crosshatching · engraving · etching · stipple ·
woodcut · scratchboard · line-texture shading · fine detail linework ·
realistic rendering.

**Banned — digital gloss:** gradients · drop shadows · glows · blur · pure
`#FFFFFF` or `#000000` · bevels · glassmorphism · stock 3D · lens flare.

**Required in every frame:**
1. Uniform-weight outlines on everything. No tapering calligraphic strokes.
2. Flat fills only. If a shape needs shading, it gets a **second flat shape**
   in a darker tone — never a ramp, never dots.
3. Paper grain over the frame, ~10% opacity, never animated.
4. Accent plate 1px off register. Subtle — it should read as cheap printing,
   not as a glitch effect.
5. Shapes simple enough that the character is redrawable frame to frame.

## Palette — four plates
| Plate | Hex | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | `#F4ECDC` | The background. Warm newsprint. Never white. |
| `ink` | `#17140F` | Line work, type, structure. Soft black, never `#000`. |
| `flame` | `#F2622E` | **Primary.** Quantity, flow, load, anything moving or filling. |
| `brass` | `#D9A520` | Secondary warm. Highlights, the coffee, Seeti's body. |
| `teal` | `#107E7D` | Healthy, passing, resolved. The only cool ink. |

Rule: `flame` is reserved for **the thing the viewer should be counting**.
If everything is orange, nothing is. `teal` only ever means "this is fine".

## Type
- **Wordmark / titles:** a condensed grotesque, heavy, tight — set as if
  letterpressed. Slight ink spread on the edges.
- **All technical text, HUD, labels, data:** monospace. Always.
- **Captions:** heavy geometric sans, burned in, 3-5 words, karaoke highlight
  (spoken words solid `ink`, upcoming words at 35%), on a `paper` slab with a
  hard `ink` border — never a soft rounded box.

## Logo
**The logo is Sundaresan's face.** Not an abstract mark — the host's portrait.
A persona channel is recognised by a person, and a face outperforms a symbol in
a feed of thumbnails.

**Mark:** head-and-shoulders portrait of Sundaresan, front facing, raising his
**dabara** near his chin. The steam off the coffee does not dissipate — it
resolves into **three connected nodes** above his head, a small system
topology. Face and architecture in one mark.

**Derived, not designed first:** the character sheet is the source of truth.
The logo is cropped and simplified from it, so the avatar and the character on
screen are unmistakably the same person.

**Wordmark:** `THE JAVA SUNDARESAN`, stacked three lines, condensed heavy,
left-aligned, `ink` on `paper`.

**Lockups:**
1. Mark left + wordmark stacked right — banner, thumbnails.
2. Mark alone in a `paper` circle with an `ink` keyline — avatar.
3. `TJS` monogram in mono — the in-video corner bug, present every frame at 55%.

**Rules:** never on white · never gradient · never rotate the mark · the steam
nodes are always exactly three · mark is never smaller than 40px.
