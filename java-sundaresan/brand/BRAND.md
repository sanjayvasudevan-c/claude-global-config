# THE JAVA SUNDARESAN — Brand Bible

## The one-line
System design, taken apart on a workbench, by an engineer with a filter coffee
and a pressure cooker that will not stop screaming.

- **Java** — coffee first, language second. The channel runs on filter coffee,

  anyone has to explain.
- **Sundaresan** — a real name. A persona brand can't be copyright-struck,

- **The** — turns a name into a title. It's a byline, not an introduction.

## Art direction — "risograph technical manual"
The look is **printed matter, not screen matter.** A 1970s engineering manual
run on a risograph: cheap warm paper, three or four spot inks, halftone dots
doing all the shading, and the accent plate landing about a pixel off register.

This is the whole differentiation strategy. Generic AI video and web-editor
output share one signature: clean vector, soft gradients, drop shadows, pure
white or pure black. Every one of those is banned here.

**Banned:** gradients · drop shadows · glows · blur · pure `#FFFFFF` or
`#000000` · bevels · glassmorphism · stock 3D · lens flare · rounded-corner
"card" UI.

**Required in every frame:**
1. Paper grain over the entire frame, ~10-14% opacity, never animated.
2. All shading is **halftone dots or hatching**. No smooth ramps, anywhere.
3. The accent plate sits 1-2px off from the ink plate. Misregistration is the
   signature — it is the thing that cannot be mistaken for a render.
4. Line weights are chunky and uneven, as if printed, not hairline-crisp.
5. Flat spot inks only. A colour is either down or it isn't.

## Palette — four plates
| Plate | Hex | Carries |
|---|---|---|
| `paper` | `#F4ECDC` | The background. Warm newsprint. Never white. |
| `ink` | `#17140F` | Line work, type, structure. Soft black, never `#000`. |
| `flame` | `#F2622E` | **Primary.** Quantity, flow, load, anything moving or filling. |
| `brass` | `#D9A520` | Secondary warm. Highlights, the coffee, Seeti's body. |
| `teal` | `#107E7D` | Healthy, passing, resolved. The only cool ink. |

Rule: `flame` is reserved for **the thing the viewer should be counting**.
If everything is orange, nothing is. `teal` only ever means "this is fine".

## Type
- **Wordmark / titles:** a condensed grotesque, heavy, tight — set as if
  letterpressed. Slight ink spread on the edges.
- **All technical text, HUD, labels, data:** monospace. Always.
- **Captions:** heavy geometric sans, burned in, 3-5 words, karaoke highlight
  (spoken words solid `ink`, upcoming words at 35%), on a `paper` slab with a
  hard `ink` border — never a soft rounded box.

## Logo
**Mark:** a South Indian filter-coffee **dabara** (tumbler seated in its bowl),
drawn flat in `ink` and `brass`. The steam rising off it does not dissipate —
it resolves into **three connected nodes**, a small system topology. Coffee
becomes architecture.

**Wordmark:** `THE JAVA SUNDARESAN`, stacked three lines, condensed heavy,
left-aligned, `ink` on `paper`.

**Lockups:**
1. Mark left + wordmark stacked right — banner, thumbnails.
2. Mark alone in a `paper` circle with an `ink` keyline — avatar.
3. `TJS` monogram in mono — the in-video corner bug, present every frame at 55%.

**Rules:** never on white · never gradient · never rotate the mark · the steam
nodes are always exactly three · mark is never smaller than 40px.
